Overview redesign — Figma frame, with fixes applied

Starts from the list-row account pattern in the shared Figma frame (node 391:12378) — genuinely better than a pill rail once an account list has to scale to corporate — and corrects what the critique flagged, rather than replacing the direction.

Balance and accounts are one card. No separate boxed "Accounts" panel — rows live directly under the balance, so the total and its breakdown aren't torn apart by a heading and a border.
Rows filter, they don't navigate away. Tapping Savings or Current narrows the balance and the activity feed below, matching how the shipped rail behaves. "View all accounts" is a separate link for drilling into the Accounts page.
Each account shows its own balance. Fixed the duplicated "GH₵ GH₵18,070.50" on both rows — Savings and Current now total correctly.
Dropped the US flag on a GH₵ account. Analytics now reads the same selection as the account rows above it, instead of carrying a mismatched currency icon.
Analytics numbers are real money. "416 / 600 / -184" → GH₵ with thousands separators, matching every other number on the page. Kept the frame's "net cashflow" stat — it was a good addition, just unformatted.
Cut the 5-tab time-range switcher from the home glance. That's exploration, not a status check — it now belongs on Reports. Cards and Analytics sit side-by-side to cut scroll depth instead of stacking full-width.
Cards stack shows 2, not 3. Dropped the leftover third card — the relationship only has a Visa debit and a Mastercard virtual.
Corporate slots are reserved, not assumed. Approvals band placeholder shown at low opacity below; a 4th "Bulk pay" square action demonstrates the action row can flex to four without a redesign.

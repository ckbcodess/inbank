Activate: the passport modal and the Truly You window

Result

Start on the Ghana Card screen and tap Use passport. The Truly You window opens over the page, like a “Continue with Google” window. On desktop you can drag it by its title bar; on a phone it fills the screen, the way a browser opens a sign-in window there. Close it with × to see what the page says when it’s closed early. “Result” sets what Truly You and our account match return.

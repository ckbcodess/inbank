Transaction history: 10 calmer ways

The moment: someone checking "did that go through, and where did my money go?", often a little worried. The one action: find a payment and know its status at a glance. What every version here drops: the tinted tile per kind, the corner direction badges, green on every credit, and the boxed card around the list. Direction is the + sign. A word appears only when a payment isn't Completed, and it is amber, never red. Totals and counts are worked out from the ledger below, not typed in.
